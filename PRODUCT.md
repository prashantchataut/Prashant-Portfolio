# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary: anyone curious about Prashant and the things he builds — students discovering NEBians, peers, and people browsing his work out of interest. Secondary: recruiters, collaborators, and founders who may evaluate him as a builder. Context is a first visit on phone or laptop; they want to understand who he is and what he ships within a few seconds.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio that introduces Prashant Chataut through his work and presence — not age, gimmicks, or ego. Success is when a visitor leaves thinking he is a cool, innovative person who builds real products, and they know how to try those products, explore his code, or get in touch.

## Positioning

A curious builder who ships innovative products people can actually use.

## Conversion & proof

- Primary and secondary CTA: browse projects with equal weight across try NEBians, contact, GitHub / Retra, and socials — no single funnel gate.
- The line a visitor remembers after 10 seconds: he is a cool person who builds innovative things.
- Belief ladder: this is a real person → he builds useful products → NEBians / Retra / Prody prove it → I can try, follow, or reach out.
- Proof on hand: NEBians live site (https://nebians.consica.com.np/), Play Store listing, Instagram (@the_nebians), app screenshots; Retra GitHub (https://github.com/prashantchataut/Retra); Prody as an active personal product.

## Brand Personality

Curious, hands-on, warm — with innovative, direct, and approachable energy. Calm and work-first, personally warm, confidently sharp without showing off. Voice stays human and careful: no slurs, no roast humor, no age-flex.

## Anti-references

Teen age-flex / gimmick portfolios (pronunciation roast games, birthday overlays as the main act). Generic dark “dev” templates with purple glow. Overly corporate recruiter-resume sites. Vibecode framing. Anything that makes the person sound egoistic or cringe.

## Design Principles

- **Work and self first.** Projects and presence carry the page; age is never the story.
- **Show, don’t tell.** Real product screenshots and shipping links beat slogans.
- **Warm without gimmicks.** Personality through tone and craft, not interactive stunts or insults.
- **Preserve what already works.** Keep the current typography and overall visual language unless a change clearly serves clarity.
- **Careful by default.** Accessible contrast, reduced motion, and respectful copy are non-negotiable.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA as the baseline: body text contrast ≥4.5:1, visible focus, keyboard access, descriptive alt text, and `prefers-reduced-motion` respected. Be careful with motion, copy tone, and any interactive features so the site stays welcoming rather than performative.
